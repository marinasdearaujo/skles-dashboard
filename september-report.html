September · What did not work

Three things worth saying plainly.

Four webinar sessions, zero attendees

Four Zoom sessions ran in September. Not one had a real attendee other than the host. Two were on-demand tests, two were live. This is the clearest signal in the whole month and it is why the funnel was rebuilt around a recorded VSL instead of a live session.

Email went quiet

502 emails sent across the month against 1,138 in August. Thirteen broadcasts went out and every one of them was a webinar notice, to a median of six people. The open rates look excellent but they are the arithmetic of tiny lists, not an improvement.

Facebook fell back

Page engagements 508 against 2,167, page views 121 against 335. August was inflated by a paid retargeting campaign that drove 1,127 of those engagements on its own, so the drop is less dramatic than it reads. The underlying organic number is small either way.

Why we are showing you this: the webinar result is the single most useful thing September produced. It cost almost nothing to learn and it redirected the entire October build.

October · The plan

Four things, in order of what they are worth.

  • Fix deliverability, then test the emails. Authenticate both sending domains this week. Everything downstream of the form is worthless while nine in ten leads never see a message.
  • Call the nine. Rodney has the list, oldest first. The person at the top has been waiting twelve days and has already seen the price without buying. A conversation tells us more than another email.
  • Work the 27 October window. Live training access ends that day. Three Apprentices, three personal calls, at the price we agreed. This is where the month's revenue actually sits.
  • Spend into what is working. At $7 a lead the new campaign is performing. The budget question is no longer whether, it is how fast we can raise it without losing the cost.
