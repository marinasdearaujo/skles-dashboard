Your call queue, start to finish

Every lead the ads bring in now lands in HubSpot with a phone number and a note on how soon they want to start. This is the whole routine, seven steps.

Who to call, right now
The live list with names and numbers is not published on this page, because this address is public. Marina sends it to you directly.

  1. Get in

    Go to app.hubspot.com and sign in as info@seanklangstonenterprises.com. That is the Skills Up account, not a personal one.

    On a new device HubSpot emails a one-time code to that same inbox, so you need to be able to open it. If you cannot, tell me before you try, and we will sort the access first rather than lock the account.

  2. Open your list

    Top menu, CRM → Lists, then open Rodney · Call queue. That list is the only place you need to look, and it updates itself.

    Sort by First touch date, oldest at the top. The person who has been waiting longest is the one to call first.

  3. Read the card before you dial

    Click a name. Four things are worth a glance: the phone number, Timing (what they said about when they want to start), Source ad (which ad they answered), and First touch date.

    Everyone in this list chose "in the next 3 months" themselves. Nobody here is browsing.

  4. Call, and open with a question

    What they signed up for was a free five minute training, not an offer. The call should end at the Apprentice, just not open there.

    What we need from these first ones is the thing the numbers cannot tell us: they said they want to start within three months, it costs $8.99, and they still have not taken it. Something is in the way and we do not know what. So ask what made them look now, then what they expected to find, then what is holding them back from starting.

    Once we have heard that five or six times we will know whether it is price, trust, format or timing, and that becomes the script.

  5. Set the lead status

    On the contact, set Lead status. This is the one field that has to be filled in, because it is what moves people out of your list.

    Attempted to Contactno answer, stays in the queue Connectedyou spoke, leaves Bad Timinginterested, not now, leaves Unqualifiednot a fit, leaves

    So a no-answer keeps coming back to you until you reach them, which is what you want.

  6. If they are interested, create a deal

    On the contact record, right side, Deals → Add. Name it after the person, put the amount in, and pick the stage. That is what makes them show up in the numbers Sean sees.

    Leadspoken to, interested, nothing bought yet
    Apprenticethey joined at $8.99
    Identifiedworth a Protégé conversation
    Invited to trialinvited into a live training
    Call with Seanbooked or happened
    Offer madeprice given, deciding
    Closed won / lostthe answer

  7. Log the call

    On the contact, Log activity → Call, and write two lines on what they said. Not for admin. It is so the next conversation starts where the last one ended, by you or by Sean.

    Write down what they tell you about why they have not started. That is the part we are actually collecting.

Two things to know

You are signing in to the shared Skills Up account, because the plan allows two logins and both are in use. Work the contacts and the deals freely, just leave the account settings alone so nobody gets logged out.

Anyone who said "just learning for now" never enters the queue, so you are not calling browsers.
