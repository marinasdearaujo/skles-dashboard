How the statuses work

Not called yet is where every lead starts. Left a message and Texted mean you tried and did not reach them, so they stay in the queue. Talked means you spoke to them. Sent the link means they have the Apprentice link. Wants in is the one that matters: it tells Sean who to expect. Not a fit and No answer, done trying take them out of the count.
