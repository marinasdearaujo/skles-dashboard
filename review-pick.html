Five ways to read the month

Same numbers, same read, same boxes to type in. What changes is how you move through it. Open each one, they all run on live November data.

Option one

Read it through

One page, no clicking. Scroll from the five headline figures down through every front, the read, the direction, your boxes and the plan. A rail down the left tracks where you are and jumps you anywhere.

Suits the 26th, when you want the whole month in one pass, and anyone who would rather print it or read it on a phone than learn a layout.

Open it →
Option two

The console

Two panes that never scroll the page. Every front sits in a list on the left with its headline number and a red or green dot; the right pane shows that one in full. Arrow keys walk down the list.

Suits working the numbers rather than reading them: you land on the red dot, open it, and nothing else is in the way.

Open it →
Option three

The board

Everything on one screen. Five dark figures across the top, a tile per front below with its three numbers, the read running down the right as a feed. Click a tile and the full list of figures slides in from the side.

Suits a glance between calls, and the month-to-month chips along the top mean you can flick through September, October and November in three clicks.

Open it →
Option four

The cycle

The page is the process. Four steps with their dates and their owner: the numbers on the 26th, our read the same day, your read on the 27th and 28th, the plan on the 29th. One step at a time, Back and Next.

Suits Marissa and Valerie, because the page tells them what is being asked of them and when, instead of expecting them to know. Opens where you left off.

Open it →
Option five

The brief

Eleven closed lines, each carrying its headline number and its verdict in a single row. Open only what you want to argue with. One button opens or closes the lot, and it prints as it stands.

Suits the fourth month, when you know the shape and only want the figure that moved. The densest of the five, and the fastest to scan.
