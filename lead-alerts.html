Opening the Skills Up inbox

Where the messages and comments from the ads are, and how to get to them. Four steps, about five minutes.

1 Accept the invite

Meta has emailed you an invitation

It goes to theaccgrp@gmail.com and comes from Facebook. It invites you into the Skills Up business account. Open it and press the button.

Respond to invite
Not in your inbox? Check spam and promotions. Invitations from Facebook land there often. The invite stays valid until 6 December.
2 Wait for access

You will be added to the Skills Up page

Accepting the invite puts you in the business account. Being added to the page itself is a separate switch, and Sean does it once you have accepted.

You will get messages and comments on the Skills Up page and nothing else, so there is no way to change the ads or the billing by accident.

3 Switch to the right page

Check the name at the top left

Business Suite opens on whichever account it used last. In your screenshot that was Dominion Partners, your own page, which is why the Skills Up messages were not there.

Click the name in the top left corner and choose Skills Up - SKLES. Then click Inbox.

Or skip the switching, this link opens it directly business.facebook.com/latest/inbox/all?asset_id=906745769194260
4 Work the three tabs

The conversations are split across three places

People who answered the ads are spread across Messenger, Instagram and Comments. Each is its own tab, and a message in one does not show in the others, so check all three.
