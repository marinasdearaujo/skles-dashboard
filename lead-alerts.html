Why the email alerts never arrived

and why there was nothing to click

Both addresses on the notification list, info@langstonrealtygroup.com and info@seanklangstonenterprises.com, are confirmed. They always were. So the address was never the problem and there was never a confirmation link waiting to be clicked.

The real reason sits in how GetResponse is built. It sends those lead alerts only for people who sign up through a form that GetResponse itself hosts. Our leads come from the Meta form and travel in through Zapier, which GetResponse files as an API contact. It does not send alerts for those, by design. Their own documentation says so.

That means no address on that list was ever going to receive them, and no amount of confirming, re-confirming or swapping the address would have changed it.

The screen with the GoDaddy button. That one is DKIM authentication for langstonrealtygroup.com. It is a sending setting, used when a domain sends marketing email. The funnel does not send from that domain, so it has no effect on your leads. Nothing to do there.

What replaces it

one email per lead, straight off the form

The alert now reads the Meta form directly instead of waiting on the CRM. Every time somebody fills it in, you get one email with everything you need to make the call, so you never have to open the CRM to look up a number.

Reading it off the form rather than off the CRM is deliberate. It is what makes sure somebody like Quinton or Radford still reaches you even when the email platform refuses them.
