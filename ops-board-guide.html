How to use the board

Everything we publish lives here now: YouTube, social, and the funnel. This walks you through it end to end. Five minutes and you're set.

Open the board →

1Getting in

One time only. After this it remembers you on that device.

  1. Open the board.
  2. Pick your name from the dropdown.
  3. Type any 4–6 digit PIN you'll remember and hit enter. The first time you do this it creates your PIN. Every time after, it's just your login.
Your name isn't in the list? Tell Marina and it gets added. The PIN is only there so edits show who made them, so don't reuse a bank PIN.

3The three views

Same cards, three ways of looking at them. Use whichever suits you — switching doesn't change anything.

  • List — closest to the old spreadsheet. Every piece as a row, sorted by date. Best for scanning the month.
  • Calendar — the month laid out. Webinars and meetings are marked. Use the arrows to move between September and October.
  • Kanban — four columns showing where each piece stands. Drag a card between columns to move it along.

4How a piece of content moves

Left to right. This is the whole workflow.

ColumnMeans
IdeasSomeone suggested it. Nothing committed, no date needed.
To ProduceApproved and being made — filming, editing, writing, designing.
ScheduledFinished and queued to go out. Nothing left to do.
PublishedLive.
Drag your own cards across as you go. If everyone keeps their column honest, nobody has to ask for a status update.

5Opening a card

Single click, anywhere on it.

Inside you get the full brief: the hook, the copy, the caption, the visual direction, the channels, the CTA, who owns it, and the dates. Edit anything, then Save.

  • Comments — feedback goes here, on the card itself, so it isn't buried in email. Everyone sees it.
  • Attachments — drop the actual file in, up to 900KB. Anything bigger, paste a Drive or Canva link instead.

6Publish date vs. produce by

The part that keeps video from going out late.

Each card carries two dates:

  • Publish date — when it goes out.
  • Produce by — when filming and editing have to be finished. The board fills this in automatically based on format, and you can override it.

A small tag on the card counts down the runway. It reads 12d runway while there's room, turns amber at two days out, and goes red if the produce-by date has passed and the card still isn't Scheduled.

FormatLead time
Long-form walkthrough14 days
Short (cut from existing footage)4 days
Reel / carousel3 days
Repost1 day
These are starting numbers, not rules. If a walkthrough realistically needs three weeks, set the date to three weeks and the board follows you.

7The 70 · 20 · 10 meter

Keeps the mix deliberate instead of accidental.

Every card is tagged one of three ways:

  • Proven (70%) — fresh content close to what already works for us.
  • Repurpose (20%) — re-running our best past posts, or recutting long-form into Shorts.
  • Experiment (10%) — genuinely new bets. A new lead magnet, a new format, an A/B test.

The meter shows where we actually are against those targets. Click any segment to filter down to just those cards.

8A few things worth knowing

  • It saves by itself. There's no publish step — hit Save on a card and everyone has it within a few seconds.
  • Everyone sees everything. There are no private cards, so if you'd rather something wasn't on the board, keep it in email.
  • Nothing on it is locked. September and October are a starting draft. Move things, kill things, add things.
  • Found something clunky? There's a Feedback tab. That's the backlog for the board itself — use it.
