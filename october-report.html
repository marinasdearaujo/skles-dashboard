Where we are

The funnel is built, and it is pulling.

October ran all thirty one days, where September ran eleven. The creative improved sharply, click-through went from 7.66% to 11.77%, and that improvement arrived in the same month the cost of reaching people on Meta nearly doubled. We absorbed almost all of it: cost per lead moved sixty cents, from $6.42 to $7.02.

Below the form the picture is thinner. The training page drew modest traffic, almost nobody reached the end of the video, and there were no sales. We also found our own page tracking is unreliable, so November starts by fixing what we measure and by phoning the twenty one people who already told us they are ready.

The zero is certain. The Purchase event live on the post-payment page since 2 October never fired, HubSpot shows no closed deal, and no new member joined the Apprentice or Protégé lists in GetResponse. Three systems, one answer.
The checkout itself is not broken. We opened both Stripe links in a clean browser this week. They load, price correctly and accept card, Apple Pay and Link. Nothing technical is blocking a sale.
The on-site counts are approximate, and we will not dress that up. The page events double-fire in places and a build session on 21 October left twenty one false checkout clicks in the data. We have stripped those out here.
So the honest read is narrow: the training page is getting modest traffic, almost nobody reaches the end of the video, and buy clicks are in low single figures. Fixing the measurement is the first job, because the next decision depends on trusting it.

October · Email and organic

Steady underneath, and built the way it should be.

Email

  • The Fast Track sequence is landing. 104 emails in October to the 38 leads on the list, opened by 10 of them. For cold paid traffic that is a working open rate.
  • It is pointed where it should be. All five emails carry a single call to action, straight to the training video, with no competing links to distract from it. The sequence is built correctly.
  • The September worry is closed. We had reported only two leads ever opening a message. With a full month of data it reads as ten of thirty eight, and both sending domains check out clean on SPF and DMARC.
  • Clicks are the number to watch next. One click came out of October. On a list of thirty eight that is too small to call either way yet, so the sequence earns its judgement in November on real volume.

Organic

  • Worth stating plainly: this is not our scope. The Instagram and YouTube content is produced and published by your side, and we have no part in the calendar and no sight of what goes out. The figures here are observation, not a StealthEnomics scorecard.
  • Quality held exactly. Engagement rate 18.3% against 18.4% in September. Reach fell 36%, and interactions fell by the same 36%, so nothing got worse about the content itself.
  • The rhythm held: 19 Instagram posts and 30 YouTube uploads, with 1,831 channel views, 5 new subscribers and 232 new Instagram followers.
  • Where it touches our funnel: the Instagram bio link got 2 clicks all month, the same as September. Organic is reaching people the adverts are paying for, and sending almost none of them anywhere.

November · The plan

Seven things, in order of what they are worth.

  • Phone the twenty one. They are already paid for, already sorted and already sitting in the queue. This is the only route to November revenue that depends on nothing being fixed first, and the conversations will tell us what the dashboards cannot.
  • Make the page tracking trustworthy. The events on the training page fire twice in places, and a build session on 21 October left twenty one false checkout clicks in the record. Until that is clean we cannot tell a real drop-off from a counting error, and every decision below the form is a guess. This is half a day of work.
  • Switch on remarketing, now that you have approved it. The people worth chasing are the ones who already watched: 2,011 video views and 2,295 page engagements in October, plus the lead list itself. One note on sequencing: the website audience of people who reached the training page exists but Meta still reports it below the minimum size to run, so it comes online as November’s traffic builds. The Meta-side audiences are large enough to start now.
  • Split the two adverts by job. "The migraine" buys the attention at 13.8% click-through, "The objection" converts it at 37.8% and $6.14 a lead. Fund both deliberately instead of letting one creative do both jobs.

November · The plan, continued

And three that compound once those are moving.

  • Test the line that earns the click. The sequence is built right and a quarter of the list opens it, so the subject lines are doing their job. One click came out of October. With November's volume behind it, the call to action inside those five emails is the first thing worth testing properly.
  • Get us sight of the organic calendar. Nineteen Instagram posts and thirty YouTube uploads went out in October and we do not know what any of them said. That content reaches the same audience the adverts are paying for, so even read-only access would let us point it at the training video and stop the bio link wasting the traffic it earns.
  • Then raise the spend. Cost per lead has held for two months through an 85% rise in media prices, so more budget will buy more leads. Worth doing once the page below the form is measured and converting, so the extra leads land somewhere that closes.
