Skills Up · Sean K. Langston · How content gets made

One recording feeds every channel

Sean records long-form for Marissa, on topics she sends him. Everything on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn is cut from those same recordings. He is asked for video by one person, once, and the rest of the calendar derives from it.

The principle

The constraint on this account has never been ideas or editing capacity. It is Sean's time in front of a camera. So the process is built to spend that time once.

The rule

Only Marissa asks Sean to record

Anyone who needs Sean on video asks Marissa for a cut of something he has already recorded. If the thing genuinely does not exist yet, it goes on her topic list for the next session rather than becoming a separate request to him. The one standing exception is personal photography, which cannot be cut from a long-form video.

The pipeline

1

Topics go to Sean

Marissa sends the topic list, which she already does. Marina adds the funnel angle, so each topic also answers an objection the adverts and emails are already raising. The same recording then serves YouTube search and the paid funnel instead of only one of them.

Marissa, with Marina
2

Sean records long-form

Target is six to eight a month, which is Marissa's number and already agreed. Nothing changes about how he records or who he sends it to.

Sean
3

The master lands in one folder

Marissa drops the full raw recording alongside her edited long-form, named by date and topic. That folder is the single source for every other channel. Nobody cuts social content from a social post, and nothing gets rebuilt from a compressed re-upload.

Marissa
4

Valerie cuts the month from the masters

Reels, carousels, stories and the still frames all come out of the masters, against the dated cards already on the ops board. Marina says which cut comes from which recording and what each one has to prove. Valerie does not need anything from Sean to work.

Valerie, under Marina's direction
5

Every card names its source and its reach

The card records which master it was cut from, and its reach at 72 hours. That is what tells us whether a long-form topic travels as a Reel, which decides the next topic list. The loop closes back into step one.

Valerie, read by Marina
